## cl. 24(3) Quorum for general meetings
One-fifth of the total number of members or twenty-five members, whichever is less, shall form the quorum for a general meeting. If within half an hour of the time fixed for the meeting a quorum is not present, the meeting stands adjourned; the adjourned meeting may transact the business on the original agenda without a quorum.
